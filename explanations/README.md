# Explanations

Conceptual and theoretical explanations of physical phenomena.

## Structure

### `theory/`
Rigorous theoretical background:
- Physical principles involved
- Assumptions and their justifications
- Theoretical framework
- Connection to broader physics concepts
- Mathematical rigor

Use Markdown or Jupyter notebooks with proper citations.

### `intuition/`
Intuitive explanations:
- Physical intuition and analogies
- Visualizing the concepts
- Common misconceptions
- Thought experiments
- "Why" questions answered

Make complex physics accessible without sacrificing accuracy.

## Best Practices

1. **Start with intuition**, then build to theory
2. **Use analogies** carefully - note their limitations
3. **Address common confusions** explicitly
4. **Connect to everyday experience** where possible
5. **Build incrementally** from simple to complex

## Example Topics

- Energy conservation in the system
- Phase space interpretation
- Symmetries and conservation laws
- Limiting behaviors (small amplitude, large amplitude, etc.)
- Time scales and characteristic behaviors
